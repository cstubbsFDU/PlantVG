PlantVGBuild a 3D voxel finite-element model of herbaceous stem cell walls from one transverse and one longitudinal microscope slide.

Browser version of PlantVG by C.J. Stubbs and A. Benzecry, Fairleigh Dickinson University. Everything runs on your device; images are never uploaded. All units are pixels, and each input image has its own scale, so keep track of your own pixel-to-micron conversion.

1

Load the two slides

Drop an image on each box, or click a box to choose a file. You can also drop both files at once anywhere on the page; a file with "trans" in its name goes to transverse. Only the first (red) channel is used, as in the original.

2

Model parameters

A buffer of 2 × radius + wall thickness slices is added internally and cropped off at export, since the end-cap random walk hasn't settled there yet.

    3

    Check the transverse traces

    Each cell lumen should get exactly one colored outline. If the outlines sit on the wall material instead of the lumens, invert the transverse image.

    4

    Cell lengths

    Cells clipped by the top or bottom edge of the longitudinal image are dropped automatically, since their true length is unknown. Drag the handles to set the lower and upper cut on what remains.

    5

    Fit the length distribution

    The distribution is chosen in step 2. With only a few surviving cells, several distributions fit about equally well; the choice is prior knowledge, not evidence from this one slide.

    6

    Generate the voxel models

    Builds the end-cap array, then the voxel matrix cell by cell, then crops and downsamples. Each model is a multi-page TIFF that ImageJ and FIJI open directly.

    Settings have changed since these models were generated. Generate again to update them.

    Drag to spin. Scroll to zoom. Right-drag to pan.

    Slice preview

    Save the models

    Each model saves as a multi-page TIFF stack that ImageJ and FIJI open directly.
